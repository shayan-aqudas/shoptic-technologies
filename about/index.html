// about us

Software that respects
the people who use it

We are an independent product company. We don’t take client work: we design, build and run our own products, today Nooraya and shoptic.store, with more on the way.

01 // our mission

Fast, beautiful and respectful of your attention.

Our starting point is simple: digital products should be fast, a pleasure to look at, and respectful of the user’s attention and privacy.

Too many apps trade experience for aggressive tracking, battery-draining background work and cluttered screens. We build products that deliver calm, precision and genuine usefulness instead, whether it is an app on your phone or a watch from shoptic.store.

02 // the nooraya journey

Our flagship, built and maintained in-house.

Nooraya was created to change how Muslims around the world connect with daily prayer, the Holy Quran and authentic Islamic literature.

A bespoke, tactile interface, accurate astronomical calculations and offline-ready audio caching make it the clearest example of what our studio stands for.

See Nooraya
03 // engineering principles

Four rules we never break.

Smooth by default

Every animation and layout is profiled on real devices so transitions stay fluid, without dropped frames.

Privacy by default

Calculations and sensor data stay on the user’s device wherever possible. We never sell personal data.

Tactile craftsmanship

Translucency, depth and careful motion that make software feel calm and physical to use.

Active stewardship

Continuous testing, timely Android platform updates and quick, human support after launch.
