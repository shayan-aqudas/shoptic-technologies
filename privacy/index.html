01Introduction

Welcome to Shoptic Technologies (“we,” “our,” or “us”). We build and maintain mobile applications and services, including our flagship application Nooraya.

We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines what information our applications access, how that information is used, and the choices you have regarding your data. By using our applications, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

02Location Information (Nooraya)

Our application Nooraya may request access to your device’s geographical location (Approximate Location and/or Precise Location).

Why location is needed:

  • Astronomical Prayer Time Calculations: Islamic prayer timings (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) vary significantly based on latitude and longitude coordinates.
  • Qibla Compass Direction: To calculate the precise azimuth angle toward the Kaaba in Makkah from your current position.

Privacy Guarantee: Your location coordinates are processed locally on your device or via ephemeral lookup queries. We DO NOT store your historical location trails on our servers, and we NEVER sell or share location data with data brokers.

03Device Permissions & Audio Features

  • Notifications: Used strictly to deliver timely prayer calls (Adhan alerts) and daily spiritual reminders. You can enable or disable notifications at any time in system settings.
  • System Settings / Ringtone Modification: If you explicitly choose to apply a sacred ringtone, adhan, or alarm sound through Nooraya’s Ringtone module, the app requests the standard Android system permission to set the chosen tone.
  • Sensors (Magnetometer / Accelerometer): Used solely for the real-time Qibla compass needle alignment.

04Third-Party Services & Analytics

To maintain app performance, track critical crashes, and support development, we partner with industry-standard service providers:

Google Play Services & Firebase Analytics: Provides anonymous crash diagnostics, app stability metrics, and aggregate installation telemetry. No personal identities are linked.

Google AdMob: May display advertisements in the free tier of our applications. AdMob may utilize anonymous advertising identifiers subject to Google’s privacy policies. Users can upgrade to Nooraya Gold for an ad-free experience.

05Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our applications are general audience apps and do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove such data.

06Data Retention & Your Rights

Since our applications operate on a privacy-first model, the majority of your user preferences (bookmarks, reading progress, notification settings) are stored locally on your device’s internal SQLite database. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the application storage in your Android device settings or uninstalling the app.

07Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted on this URL.
